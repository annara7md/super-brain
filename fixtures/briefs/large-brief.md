# Program Goal
Deliver an integrated platform that unifies patient scheduling, telehealth sessions, claims processing, and retail pharmacy fulfillment.

Clinical Workflow:
Clinicians need appointment triage queues, urgent escalation paths, asynchronous follow-up messaging, and prescription refill visibility.

Insurance and Revenue Cycle:
Claims must be generated from encounter records, validated against payer rules, and submitted with denial management feedback loops.

Commerce and Inventory:
Retail operations require near real-time stock counts, substitution rules for shortages, and regional fulfillment fallback strategies.

Data and Integrations:
The system must connect with EHR vendors, payment processors, video conferencing APIs, and logistics providers using audited integration contracts.

Security Expectations:
Teams expect SSO, least-privilege role templates, immutable audit trails, and proactive anomaly detection for privileged actions.

Missing Details:
No explicit accessibility standard, no objective on peak concurrency, no definition of acceptable outage windows, and no finalized rollout sequence by region.
